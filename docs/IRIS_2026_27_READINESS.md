# IRIS 2026–27 Readiness

This is an internal compliance and engineering checklist. It is not a research plan, abstract, poster, or submission draft.

## Confirmed official requirements

- IRIS is open to Indian-origin students in Classes 5–12 who reside and study in India.
- A project may be submitted by one student or a team of exactly two students.
- HYDRA fits the Computational Biology and Bioinformatics category.
- The official IRIS page currently asks for a project synopsis, full research paper, and 90-second video.
- IRIS requires original research supported by data; an essay or literature-only project is not eligible.
- IRIS materials must not disclose the student's school, city, or state.
- Public, pre-existing, de-identified datasets are exempt from ISEF human-participant preapproval, but the source databases and original studies still need to be documented.
- The 2026–27 ISEF rules allow AI as a cited project resource, but prohibit generative AI from writing the research plan, abstract, poster, or citations. The student must author and understand all submission material.
- ISEF research for the 2027 fair must fall within one continuous 12-month period beginning no earlier than January 2026.

## Items that require confirmation from IRIS

The current IRIS page says registration opens in September 2026, but it also lists August 15 and October 15 submission windows. Those statements conflict, so the team should obtain written confirmation of the actual 2026–27 opening date and deadlines.

IRIS also says experimental data cannot be more than 12 months old. HYDRA performs new analysis during 2026 on older public TCGA/GEO/ArrayExpress source data. The team should ask the IRIS Scientific Review Committee whether this satisfies the rule and retain their written answer.

## Project records to preserve

- A dated, student-written research notebook explaining every decision and result.
- Git history showing when code, analyses, and interpretations changed.
- Exact public-data accessions, download dates, checksums, and source-study links.
- A contribution log separating student work, mentor advice, and AI/tool assistance.
- All original and current outputs; negative external validation results must remain visible.
- Any required ISEF forms signed before work that requires preapproval. Public-data analysis appears exempt, but the Adult Sponsor and fair SRC make the final determination.

## Submission quarantine

`paper/main.tex` is a development draft and must not be submitted to IRIS/ISEF. The student must independently write the research plan, abstract, poster, citations, and video script in their own words after the results are frozen.
