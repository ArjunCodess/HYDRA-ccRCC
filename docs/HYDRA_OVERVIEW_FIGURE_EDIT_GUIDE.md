## Verified wording

The supplied SVG labels external direction counts as unadjusted, with 21/22 and 5/21. Harmonized clinical adjustment is a separate assessment reported in the paper.

The supplied SVG states 'The joint ridge predictor improves held-out ranking within TCGA.' Ridge estimates clinical and gene coefficients together, so its contrast does not isolate the contribution of individual genes.
